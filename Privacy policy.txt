Privacy Policy - WiRoc Config
Last updated: 11 September 2026

WiRoc Config is an app for configuring WiRoc devices (SportIdent timing and
registration equipment). This policy explains what information the app
accesses, what it stores on your device, and what it sends over the network.

In short: WiRoc Config has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics,
crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not sell your information.
The app talks to WiRoc devices over Bluetooth and, where relevant, to WiRoc's
own server (monitor.wiroc.se) to look up firmware updates and to manage device
access.

1. Permissions the app requests
   - Bluetooth: used to find and connect to nearby WiRoc devices.
   - Location (Android): the Android operating system requires location
     permission for apps that scan for Bluetooth devices. The app does not use
     your location for any other purpose and does not track or store it.
   - Network/Internet: used to communicate with WiRoc devices on your local
     network and to reach monitor.wiroc.se.

2. Information read from WiRoc devices
   When you scan for and connect to a WiRoc device, the app reads information
   from that device, including its Bluetooth address (the device identifier),
   its name, its signal strength (RSSI), and its configuration settings. This
   information is used only to display and configure your device.

3. Information stored on your device
   The app keeps a list of the WiRoc devices you have used, storing for each
   device its name, Bluetooth address and, if you use it, the network address of
   the device's REST API. This is kept in local storage on your phone and is
   removed when you delete the device in the app or uninstall the app.
   The app also keeps a short in-app log (the last 500 messages) for
   troubleshooting. This stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

4. Information sent to WiRoc's server (monitor.wiroc.se)
   - Firmware update lookup: when you open the Update screen for a connected
     device, the app sends the device's hardware version, hardware revision and
     Bluetooth address to monitor.wiroc.se in order to retrieve the list of
     available firmware versions. No personal information is included.
   - Device access: if you use the "Device access" screen, the email address and
     password you enter (for your monitor.wiroc.se account) are sent over HTTPS
     to monitor.wiroc.se in order to grant that account access to the device.
     The app does not store your password.
   monitor.wiroc.se is operated by the WiRoc project. Information sent there is
   used only for the purposes above (firmware lookup and device access
   management) and is not used for advertising or profiling.

5. Tailscale (optional)
   If you enable the Tailscale VPN feature for a device, the app opens the
   device's Tailscale login link in your web browser. Signing in is handled by
   Tailscale in the browser; the app does not receive or store your Tailscale
   credentials. If you tap "Copy link", the link is placed on your clipboard.

6. Third parties
   The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking
   services, and does not sell or rent your information.

7. Children's privacy
   The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
   information from children.

8. Changes to this policy
   We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the
   "last updated" date above.

9. Contact
   Questions about this policy can be sent to info(at)wiroc.se.
